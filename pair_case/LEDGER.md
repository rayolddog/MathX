| L17 | 2026-09-08 | "Entanglement = equal phases" (JB, 2026-09-06) is, in quantum mechanics, a product state with a shared classical variable, and its correlations under any local rule are those of Bell's local models: the sawtooth 1 − 4θ/π for the projection rule (CHSH 2.000) and ½ cos 2θ for the sampled rule (1.414), against the measured cos 2θ (2.83, Aspect 1982 onward). The measured correlations are those of the joint two-photon amplitudes, which the model does not carry. | D1, D3, Tables 1 and 2 |
| L18 | 2026-09-08 | The model's only route above Bell's bound is its phase-dependent efficiency (SPAD study, Part D) acting as a detection gate, which puts the coincidence correlations on the Garg–Mermin line 4/η_c − 2 and reaches the quantum value at η_c = 0.828. This is the detection loophole, closed by the loophole-free experiments of 2015 (Hensen et al., Giustina et al., Shalm et al.). A rule that reached the measured correlations at full detection would have to condition the outcome at one analyzer on the setting at the other, which no rule local to each analyzer can do. | D4, Table 3, C2, C4 |
| L19 | 2026-09-08 | The single-photon Bell experiments use one-photon absorption at each detector (first order, linear in intensity, local), and the pair is made by a second-order process at the source (parametric down-conversion, or a cascade). So the detector step of this program can be exactly the local deposit the SPAD study built, and the correlations cannot be made there; JB's observation of 2026-09-08 that the single-photon Bell experiment "eliminated the second process" at the detector is recorded as agreed. | §0 |
| L20 | 2026-09-08 | JB's option 2 (both detectors' first spinors synchronized to one bulk phase, the second spinors rotating with them, no communication) is a common-cause model: the bulk phase is a shared variable fixed before the settings, the outcomes are cos²(μ + δ − a) and cos²(μ + δ − b), and the correlations are those of Tables 1 and 2 (CHSH 2.00 or 1.41). No violation. Option 1 (particle 2's phase set by the outcome at A) gives cos 2θ and 2.83 with Malus at B and is the projection postulate applied to the pair; it is nonlocal without signalling. A bulk that carried A's outcome to B would be option 1 with the bulk as the carrier, excluded at subluminal speed by the spacelike-separated experiments. | D7, D8, Table 4 |
