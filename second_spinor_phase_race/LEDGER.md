| L1 | 2026-09-07 | The internal offset δ_p, carried through the contact and able to decide the record, is a variable local to the particle and the detector in Bell's sense. | L3 | 2026-09-07 | The one detector with a phase-ordered bulk that the record examined, the superconducting nanowire (record contract G3), commits by a thermal hotspot with no dependence on the condensate phase.
